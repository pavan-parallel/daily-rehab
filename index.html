THE SIMPLE RULES

A little, consistently.

  • Leave a few reps.Finish with 3–4 good reps still possible. Keep more in reserve as you restart.
  • Move comfortably.No forcing a stretch or pushing through a painful catch.
  • Check tomorrow.Your usual daily movements should feel no worse.

Make it a rhythm.

Upper with upper. Lower with lower. Usually twice each per week, with a day between demanding sessions for the same area.
